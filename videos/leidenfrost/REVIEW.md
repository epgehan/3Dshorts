Every frame was decoded/measured; visual review targets probes, cuts and acting/effect changes rather than claiming exhaustive manual inspection. 448 unique source captures include two brief held captures; the encoded stream has no identical adjacent decoded frames. Word timestamps use ASR/DTW estimates, not a manual phoneme audit. The vapor flow and gap are schematic/enlarged; water vapor itself is invisible. This is original artwork following the skill, not recovered source rigs or a promise of pixel-identical reference animation.
