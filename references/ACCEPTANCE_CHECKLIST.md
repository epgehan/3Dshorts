# Acceptance checklist

For each item record `passed`, `needs revision`, or `not tested`, with frame/time evidence. Do not replace observations with an assumed score. A package-validation pass is not a rendered-video pass.

## Before production

- Brief states user instructions, original story/factual sources, format/fps, narration version and locked timing.
- Character identity sheet and facing/expression vocabulary approved or ready for review.
- Scene recipes chosen for actual settings; user subtitle policy recorded.
- Source-reference pixels are local/private and provenance/permissions recorded.

## Character / world

- Broad pale flat face, sparse features, compact torso, thin limbs and stable costume/hair identity.
- No spherical/glossy replacement face or routine bulky tubular arms.
- Hair/armor have controlled drawn volume where motivated; no unplanned anatomical creep.
- Front/back/three-quarter states make sense; no frontal face pasted on a back view.
- Hands touch props, feet touch floor, seated bodies touch seats; shadows and occlusion agree.
- Perspective, repetitions, material density and foreground/rear layering support the action.

## Lighting / post

- Face/outline readable through world-light changes; motivated color exceptions explicit.
- No universal violet shadow, orange wash, blue-multiplied face or black-crushed interior.
- Materials can differ in gloss/density; no universal matte/noisy surface preset.
- Focus follows the acting plane; alpha cards do not make rectangular blur/depth artifacts.
- Local glow stays local; subtitle overlay stays sharp/white after all post.

## Motion / editing

- Separate pose, face, limbs/props, camera and edit tracks.
- Pose holds can be deliberate; global frozen-frame metric is not a ban on still actors.
- Hard cuts, dissolves, inserts and occluder transitions classified by actual frames.
- No identical push, roll or easing duration on every shot.
- Every cut/transition boundary, contact action and major expression/effect onset inspected in adjacent frames.
- Reference feature-flow metrics are not described as physical camera meters/FOV/rig evidence.

## Captions / audio

- ALL CAPS Arial, white only, black outline; actual font loaded and no silent substitute.
- Short legible groups fit safe area and do not cover face/causal prop.
- Frame/word timestamps reviewed against exact approved voice; no drift or unauthorized audio retiming.
- No extra labels/title text without instruction.
- Final mixed audio measured; isolated voice/music levels not invented from mixed source.

## Delivery

- Final video full-decodes without errors; dimensions/fps/framecount/duration/audio endpoints confirmed.
- Final stills and motion reviewed at delivery size and native360 × 640-equivalent size.
- Report distinguishes exhaustive computation, targeted human visual review, estimates and untested parts.
- New original assets/code and licensed dependencies separated from private reference videos/frames/transcripts.
- If only delivering a skill, explicitly say a new video has not yet been rendered to prove a match.
- No claim of perfect source-pixel match, original software recovery or an unavailable named-model audit.
